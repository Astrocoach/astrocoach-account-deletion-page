Delete your Astrocoach account

You can permanently delete your Astrocoach account at any time, directly from within the app. Here's how it works and what happens to your data.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Astrocoach app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Go to Account.
  4. Open Account Settings.
  5. Tap Delete Account.
  6. Confirm your choice to permanently delete your account.

⚠ What happens when you delete

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. When you confirm:

  • Your Astrocoach account is permanently deleted.
  • Your personal profile and account information are removed.
  • All app data associated with your account is deleted.

Data retention

Some information may be retained only where required to comply with applicable laws, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, or enforce our legal obligations. Any retained information is kept only for the minimum period required and is securely deleted when it is no longer needed.

Need help?

If you can't access your account or need assistance with deletion, contact our support team at support@astro-c.com.
